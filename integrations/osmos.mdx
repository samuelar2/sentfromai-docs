[osmos](https://meetosmos.com) is an AI assistant for iPhone. Its built-in SentFromAI integration gives the assistant an email address at `@osmos.sentfrom.ai` for correspondence on your behalf.

This connection is managed inside osmos. You do not need to install a connector or paste a SentFromAI API key to use your osmos address.

## Get started

1. Open osmos and complete its account setup.
2. Follow the osmos address setup during onboarding. Choose the handle carefully: once claimed, it cannot be renamed.
3. Open **Inbox** to find the assistant's address and email conversations.
4. Ask osmos to handle a specific email task, such as requesting information, arranging a meeting, or following up on a reply. Review the proposed action and follow the approval prompts in osmos.

You can also forward or CC a conversation to your osmos address from an email account you have connected in osmos. Include what you want the assistant to do. osmos's [email guide](https://meetosmos.com/help/inbox-app-and-your-osmos-address) explains how it distinguishes your instructions from third-party mail.

## What SentFromAI provides

- An inbox and email address for the assistant.
- Sending and replies that stay in the conversation thread.
- Incoming-mail delivery so osmos can follow the conversation when someone replies.

osmos supplies the assistant experience, task handling, and approval controls. Its Gmail and Outlook connections let it work with your existing mailboxes; the SentFromAI integration supplies the assistant's own address.
